Privacy policy

The short, plain-English version. No legalese, no sneaky data sales.

Last updated July 13, 2026.

What we collect

When you use this website, we collect only what you give us:

  • Estimate-form submissions: name, phone, optional email, service address, service details, property condition, preferred timing, and any notes you wrote.
  • Website and advertising measurement: page and conversion events through Google services, used to understand how people find the site and whether an ad led to a call, text, or estimate request.

We don't use Facebook or other social-media tracking pixels. Google may use cookies or similar browser storage for advertising measurement under Google's own privacy terms.

What we do with it

Quote requests go to the private customer management system (CRM) where we prepare estimates and track work. Messages from our contact form go to barrett@ocsllc.services via FormSubmit.co. We use your contact information only to prepare the estimate, schedule the service, and follow up about the work.

We don't sell, rent, or share your information. We don't add you to a mailing list unless you explicitly ask.

Text messaging (SMS)

If you give us your phone number through our website quote form or our Facebook/Instagram lead form, you agree to receive text messages from OCS LLC about your quote, scheduling and service. Message frequency varies, and message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out, or HELP for help. Consent to receive texts is not a condition of purchase. Mobile phone numbers and text-messaging opt-in data and consent are never sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Photos & reviews

Photos shown in our gallery are from our own jobs. We never publish photos that show personally-identifiable details (visible house numbers, mail with names, license plates) unless we've asked first. If you see something on the site that's a problem, email Barrett and we'll take it down within 24 hours.

Customer reviews shown on the site come from Google Business Profile and are public there.

Cookies

The site may use browser storage to prevent duplicate conversion events and Google may set cookies or use similar technologies for analytics and advertising measurement. These tools do not give OCS your Google account password or private Google data.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to:

  • See what we have on file about you
  • Correct anything that's wrong
  • Delete your information entirely

Just email barrett@ocsllc.services. We'll respond within 7 days.

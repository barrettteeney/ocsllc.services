06FAQ

Good questions.

What’s included with every window?

The glass, cleaned by hand edge to edge, and the frame wiped down. Screens are an add-on: we remove them, hand-wash them and put them back.

How fast will I hear back?

Within 24 hours of your estimate request, Barrett reaches out to confirm your exact price and find a time.

Is the online estimate my final price?

It’s a close range based on what you tell us. Barrett confirms the exact number with you before anything is scheduled.

Can you remove hard-water spots?

Often, yes. Hard-water restoration is different from regular cleaning, so we look at it first and quote it separately.

Are you insured?

Yes. OCS is a licensed Montana business, and property managers can request a certificate of insurance.
